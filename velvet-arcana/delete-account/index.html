Delete your account

Velvet Arcana · Operated by FluxoGen
In short
  • You can delete your account yourself, inside the App, in under a minute.
  • Deleting it removes your sign-in and the journal copy linked to it.
  • No longer have the App? Email us and we will delete it for you.

1. Delete it in the App (fastest)

  1. Open Velvet Arcana and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings then Account.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Deletion happens straight away and cannot be undone. The App also clears the journal stored on that phone.

2. Ask us to delete it by email

If you cannot open the App (for example, you uninstalled it or lost your phone), email fluxogentechnologies@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my Velvet Arcana account”. We may reply to confirm the request comes from you. We aim to complete deletion within 30 days.

Please do not send passwords or any other sensitive details by email.

3. What is deleted

  • Your sign-in: email address, password and any linked Google sign-in.
  • Your profile.
  • Readings and journal entries saved to your account, and their iterations.

4. What we may keep, and for how long

  • Usage counts that limit how many AI readings can be requested each day. They are kept for about 35 days, then removed.
  • Reports you sent about a reading. They are not linked to your account, so deleting the account does not remove them.
  • Closed-test quality log, while the closed test runs. It is keyed to an IP address rather than an account. Email us with the approximate date and time of your session and we will do what we can. It is deleted when the test ends.
  • Device backups managed by Google under your own account settings, which we cannot access.
