Free readings. Each person gets a small number of free AI readings, shown in the App. Deleting an account and creating a new one does not give new free readings (see the Privacy Policy).

Plans. A plan gives you AI readings up to the limits shown in the App when you buy, for example one daily card reading a day and a set number of spread readings each month. A spread reading together with up to three clarifying cards counts as one spread reading. Unused readings do not carry over to the next month.

  • Monthly and Yearly plans renew automatically and Google Play charges you at the start of each period until you cancel.
  • Passes (Monthly pass, Yearly pass) are one payment for one period and do not renew. If you buy a pass while a plan is still running, the new time starts when the current plan ends.
  • The App cannot switch you from one plan to another. To change plans, cancel the current one in Google Play and buy the other when it ends, or buy a pass to add time after it.

Single questions. A single question is a one-time purchase that covers one spread reading and up to three clarifying cards. It is not used for the daily card. It stays available until you use it. If you have a plan and have used this month’s spread readings, a single question you own is used for your next spread reading.

Payment. Google Play takes all payments under its own terms. Prices are shown by Google Play in your currency, with taxes added where they apply. If we change the price of a plan, Google Play tells existing subscribers before it applies to them.

Cancelling. Cancel a plan any time in Google Play (also under Settings > Plan > Manage in Google Play). You keep the plan until the end of the period you paid for, and it does not renew. Deleting your account does not cancel a plan.
