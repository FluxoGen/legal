1. What the App is

Ease is a guide to self-acupressure: pressing points on your own body with your fingers. It describes where traditional acupressure points are, how to press them, and what they are traditionally used for, and offers a guided timer.

The App is wellness education. It is not:

  • medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, or a medical device;
  • a promise that any point will relieve any symptom;
  • a substitute for a doctor, a licensed acupuncturist or emergency services.

If you have severe, sudden or unusual symptoms, contact a medical provider or local emergency services. Do not delay care to use the App.

2. Using it safely

  • Pressure should feel good, not painful. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness or anything unusual.
  • Do not press on broken skin, bruises, swelling, varicose veins or recent injuries.
  • Points marked “reference only” are not for pressing yourself.
  • If you are or might be pregnant, tell the App so it can warn you about points traditionally avoided in pregnancy, and check with your medical provider before using acupressure.
  • Talk to your medical provider first if you have a health condition, take blood thinners, or have a pacemaker or other implanted device.

3. Accuracy of the content

Point locations are written from the WHO Standard Acupuncture Point Locations (2008) and independent references, and checked by a separate review step. They have not yet been reviewed by a licensed acupuncturist, and the drawings show approximate positions. References sometimes differ, and the App says so where they do. We work to keep the content accurate but do not guarantee it is complete or error-free.
