Privacy Policy

Ease (the “App”, including the Android app and the website at ease-murex.vercel.app) · Operated by FluxoGen (“we”, “us”)
Effective date: 9 October 2026
In short
  • There are no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email or payment details.
  • The App remembers a few settings (your optional answer about pregnancy, light or dark appearance, and when you dismissed an optional prompt). They stay on your device. We never receive them.
  • The Android app does not use the internet at all. It has no internet permission.
  • No advertising. No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDKs.

1. Who we are

Ease is a self-acupressure guide made by FluxoGen. FluxoGen decides how the information described here is used. You can reach us at fluxogentechnologies@gmail.com for any privacy question or request.

2. What we handle, and why

2.1 Stored on your device

The App stores three small settings in its private storage on your device:

  • Your answer about pregnancy (pregnant or not sure, or doesn’t apply to me). It is optional. The App uses it only to set aside, or warn about, points traditionally avoided during pregnancy. You can answer from the optional card on the Home screen, from a point that needs it, or in Settings, and change it at any time in Settings.
  • Your appearance choice (System, Light or Dark).
  • The date you dismissed the optional pregnancy card with “Not now”, so it does not keep asking.

The pregnancy answer is health-related, so we keep it out of reach: none of these settings is sent to us or to anyone else, and the Android app turns off Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for its data. Uninstalling the App, or clearing its data (or your browser’s site data on the website), deletes them.

Everything else in the App (point descriptions, drawings, photos, fonts) is built into the App. The App does not record what you look at or search for, and none of it is sent to us. (On the website, a search is part of the page address, so it can appear in your own browser history.)

2.2 The Android app

The Android app asks for no permissions that need your approval, and none that reach the network: it does not include Android’s INTERNET permission, so it cannot send or receive data. It keeps the screen on while a guided press timer runs, which needs no permission.

2.3 The website

The website is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel receives standard request information (such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested) to deliver the pages and protect the service, and handles it under its own privacy policy. After your first visit the website stores its files, and the settings above, in your browser so it can work offline and remember them.

2.4 Links that leave the App

Some links open other websites, for example a point’s reference sources, the U.S. VA handouts, FluxoGen’s page and these legal pages. In the Android app they open in your phone’s browser, outside the App. Those sites have their own privacy policies and may collect information when you visit. If you use the Contact link, your email app sends us an email, and we receive your email address and what you write. We use it only to reply.

2.5 What we do not collect

  • Name, email address (unless you email us), phone number or payment details
  • Location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Analytics events, usage data or crash reports

4. Our reasons for using it

Where the law asks for a reason (a “legal basis”), we rely on providing the service you ask for (using your pregnancy answer on your device to show safe points) and your choice to answer it, to change your settings, and to contact us.

5. How long we keep it

  • On your device: until you change a setting, clear the App’s data or uninstall.
  • Emails you send us: as long as needed to answer you, then deleted on request.
  • Google and Vercel: according to their own retention.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Change your pregnancy answer and appearance in Settings at any time, or clear them by clearing the App’s data.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, India or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to processing. Email fluxogentechnologies@gmail.com and we will respond. We hold no data about you from using the App, so for most requests there is nothing on our side to retrieve.
  • If you are in the EEA or UK, you may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

7. Children

The App is intended for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children.

8. Security

Your settings, including your pregnancy answer, are held in the App’s private storage, protected by your device. Anyone with unlocked access to your device may be able to see them in the App. The website is served only over HTTPS.

9. Changes

If the App changes in ways that affect your information (for example accounts, sync or analytics), we will update this policy and the date at the top before the change, and tell you in the App for material changes. Earlier versions are listed below.
